x̂. Some information is known; the mean and covariance of x are given by

                         E[x] = m,     E[(x − m)(x − m)T ] = Q

The measurement noise v satisfies

                                E[v] = 0,   E[vvT ] = R

It is assumed that x and v are completely uncorrelated.

                                    x̂ = m + K(y − Cm)                                 (4)
The first term centers the estimate on the mean, the second term allows variations about
the mean. The measurement error will be denoted

                                        e = x − x̂

Note that the matrix K is left undetermined for now.
