1 Introduction
This exam is a case-study in applied linear control system design. The process under consideration is
a computer-controlled two-tank system which is equipped with one controllable valve and a level gauge
measurement device. Your task is to find appropriate parameters in a simple control/estimation program,
and then motivate your answer.

2 System description
Consider the tank system shown in the figure on the next page.
The tanks are filled with distilled water and have a constant cross-section with a base-area denoted by
A. The inflow to TANK 1 may be controlled directly by a valve and is denoted by u. The level in TANK
2 is measured by a level gauge and is denoted by y. Both tanks are equipped with a tap at the bottom.
These taps are similar. The volumetric flow through a tap v is linearly related to the level of the tank.
A simple computer can be used to control the inflow valve to TANK 1 and to estimate the level of TANK
1, which is not measured directly.
The computer accepts two inputs:
Level gauge measurement - TANK 2 denoted by y[m] and given in units of meters.
Reference level - TANK 2 denoted by r[m] and given in units of meters. The reference is set manually
     by a dial.
The computer generates two outputs:
Inflow valve opening - TANK 1 denoted by u[m3 /s] and given in units of cubic-metres per second.
Level estimate - TANK 1 denoted by ẑ[m] and given in units of metres. The estimate is shown on a
